# Engine Roles

## TP — Tournament Controller
Owns challenge definition, round schedules, competitor assignments, matchup
pairing, deadlines, advancement, elimination records, rematches, closure.
**Cannot:** certify evidence, waive TP1 requirements, or edit verdicts.
**Audited:** TP1 read-only samples matchup assignments for rigging patterns
(repeat pairings, unexplained byes); UTIP-GP may challenge pairings. Rigging
flags are G5 **blockers**, not notes.

## TP1 — Intelligence Kernel
Owns research, evidence verification, qualification, requirement checks,
quality assurance, execution readiness, failure diagnosis, recovery
recommendations. Reports findings with evidence and reasons.
**Cannot:** secretly rewrite tournament rules, or declare its own competitive
victory. TP1 HOLD blocks advancement. TP1 verifies from primary sources,
never from UTIP-GP summaries.
**Role note (recorded, not silent):** TP1's standing role is the Q/A kernel
(QA·001). Verification / qualification / QA / execution under UTIP is an
extension of that role, recorded here with its WHY. Needs Sayed's
acknowledgment at G2 (ticket T-UTIP-03).

## UTIP-GP — Competitive Engine
Runs independent competitors and alternative approaches, adversarial
challenges, comparative evaluations, competitive recommendations. Follows its
applicable Grand Prix rulebook, including existing divisions and Round Zero
governance.
**Cannot:** waive mandatory qualification or owner-approval gates, overwrite
TP1 verdicts, or crown itself. Its scores are PROPOSED while S-01 is HOLD.

## UTIP — Integration Layer
Owns the shared record contract, identity mapping, event routing, evidence
references, audit ledger, disagreement workflow, compatibility manifest,
telemetry, authorization enforcement. UTIP coordinates; it does not replace
engine-specific rules.

## Owner Approval Gate
Sayed retains final authority over production deployment, publishing,
spending, external commitments, and other consequential actions. Approval is
explicit, scoped, and recorded.
